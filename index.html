About Abdur Rob

Abdur Rob Miah is an experienced MEP & Interior Services provider with more than 35 years of practical experience in electrical, plumbing, interior development and renovation work.

He reviews each project based on its requirements, discusses scope, cost and timing with the client, and then handles the work according to the size and nature of the project.
